Learn B2B Sales Pipeline Analytics with Phoebe

Every Monday a pipeline number is read out and a forecast is promised. These ten sessions ask what that number is evidence of, and how wrong the forecast usually is. The work splits the way it splits in real teams. Four Business sessions are for the BD and sales leads who own the number and the commit: what a pipeline proves, how to read a pipeline review, who owns the forecast, and how to get from pipeline to a hiring plan. Six Analyst sessions are for the people who own the method and its error rate: the data model that remembers the past, conversion and cycle time done honestly, three forecast methods, how to measure their error, and a bench that scores all three against what really closed on real B2B funnel rows. The running team, Kettle & Co's office-tea arm, is constructed.

Analyst track 🧮 · six sessions, hands-on in the browser

For the data analysts who build pipeline reporting and forecasts. Every widget computes in your browser on real rows of Olist's public B2B funnel: 8,000 leads and the 842 that signed. The bench forecasts one quarter three ways and scores each against what really closed.
